# Privacy Policy — Crown Quest

_Last updated: 1 August 2026_

> **Note for the developer:** this version describes the app **with ads
> enabled**. If you ship with `AdConfig.enabled = false` in `js/config.js`,
> replace the Advertising section below with a plain statement that the app
> collects nothing — see MONETIZATION.md.

## The short version

Crown Quest does not ask for your name, email or any account. Your game
progress stays on your device. The one thing that does leave your device is
the data Google's advertising service needs in order to show ads.

## What stays on your device

Your progress — level, stars, best scores, coins, lives and whether you have
purchased Remove Ads — is saved in local storage on your device. It is not
uploaded anywhere, is not tied to any identity, and we cannot see it.

Deleting the app removes it. "Reset progress" in the in-game Settings menu
clears it immediately.

## Advertising

The game shows ads supplied by **Google AdMob**. To deliver and measure those
ads, Google's software collects a limited amount of information from your
device, which may include:

- a device advertising identifier
- general device and app information (model, OS version, app version)
- ad interaction data, such as whether an ad was shown, viewed or tapped
- coarse location inferred from your IP address

We do not receive your identity from Google, and we do not combine this
information with anything else.

Google's use of this data is governed by their own policies:

- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

By default this app requests **non-personalised ads**, meaning ads are chosen
from general context rather than a profile of your interests.

**Removing ads:** the Remove Ads purchase stops banner and between-level ads.
Rewarded videos — the optional "watch an ad" offers you choose to tap — remain
available, and showing one still involves Google as described above.

## Purchases

Remove Ads is processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment
details. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase succeeded.

## What the app never does

- No accounts, logins or sign-ups
- No analytics or crash reporting of our own
- No access to location services, contacts, camera, microphone or photos
- No selling of data to anyone
- No content you can create or share, and no messaging

## Children

The game is rated 4+ and contains no violent or adult content. It is not
directed at children under 13, is not part of the App Store Kids Category, and
we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are
requested as non-personalised by default.

## Your choices

- iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking** controls whether apps may
  request to track you.
- iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising** lets you reset or
  limit your advertising identifier.
- Buying Remove Ads stops banner and between-level ads.

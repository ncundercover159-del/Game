# Privacy Policy — Crown Quest

_Last updated: 31 July 2026_

## The short version

Crown Quest does not collect, transmit or share any data about you. The game
runs entirely on your device and has no network functionality at all.

## What the app stores

The game saves your progress — level, stars, best scores, coins and lives — to
local storage on your device. This never leaves your device, is not tied to any
identity, and cannot be seen by us or anyone else.

Deleting the app removes it. "Reset progress" in the in-game Settings menu
clears it immediately.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts, logins or sign-ups
- No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting
- No advertising and no ad identifiers
- No in-app purchases
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access
- No third-party SDKs that collect data
- No network requests of any kind — the game works with the device offline

## Children

The game is rated 4+ and is safe for children. Because it collects no data
whatsoever, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information
from children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published here and the
date above will be revised.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the address listed on the app's
App Store page.

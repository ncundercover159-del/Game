A CHRONOPHOTOGRAPH IN TWENTY EXPOSURES

  1. Twenty seconds an exposure. Twenty exposures. Hard cut, no exceptions.
  2. Then you come back. Everything you just did is developed into a ghost that repeats it, on a twenty-second loop, forever.
  3. Ghosts are solid. A storey is 2.8m. You can jump 1.8m. The only way up is to stand on somebody you used to be.
  4. There are more plates than there are of you. So leave someone useful behind. You are cooperating with your past self, who was an idiot.
  5. Blue plates are turnstiles. They only hold while weight is increasing. Standing on one is a wasted body.
  6. You are carrying a knife. Stand against a past self and press KNIFE. It is not deleted — from that instant in its loop it simply dies, and falls, and does so again every twenty seconds for the rest of the plate.

Left thumb walks. JUMP is bottom right.
There is no undo. That is the game.

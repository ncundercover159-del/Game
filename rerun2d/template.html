A RERUN IN TWENTY OBSERVATIONS

  1. You are a Lump. Twenty seconds an observation, twenty observations. Hard cut, no exceptions, no undo.
  2. Then you come back. Everything you just did is decanted into the tank as a solid past self that repeats it on a twenty-second loop, forever, alongside you.
  3. Past selves are furniture. They shove you, they never budge, and you can climb on their heads. The shelf is 2.1m up and you can only jump 1.36m, so the only way onto it is somebody you used to be.
  4. There are more plates than there are of you. Leave a useful idiot behind on each one. You are cooperating with a version of yourself who cannot hear you.
  5. Blue plates are turnstiles. They only hold while weight is increasing. Parking a specimen on one wastes it.
  6. You are carrying a knife. Stand against a past self and press KNIFE. It is not deleted — from that instant in its loop it simply dies where it stands, and does so again every twenty seconds for the rest of the experiment. It will never hold a plate again.
